What you can convert

Image converters cover JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF and other common formats. Document tools handle PDF, Word files and plain text. Audio and video converters let you change containers and pull audio out of a clip, and ebook converters move books between EPUB, PDF and other reader formats. Each converter has its own page, so you can bookmark the ones you use most often.

When it helps

Most conversions come from small, everyday problems. A form accepts only PDF, a coworker's laptop won't open HEIC, an image saved from a website arrives as WebP, or you want just the soundtrack of a video. Picking the right target format usually fixes it: JPG for photos you share, PNG for screenshots and transparency, PDF for documents meant to be read rather than edited, and MP3 for audio that has to play on any device.

For sensitive documents, check that you are comfortable uploading them to any online service. CleverUtils removes uploads automatically after 2 hours, and you can start at https://cleverutils.com/.
